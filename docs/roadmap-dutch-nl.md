# DutchBuddy — Upper Extension (Weeks 13–18, B1 → C1 gateway)

*Season 2 of the DutchBuddy content pack (`dutch-nl`). Extends the shipped 12-week
A1→B1 arc (weeks 1–12, days 1–84; see **`docs/plan.md` §3** for that curriculum)
by six weeks / 42 days (days 85–126), taking the learner from a confident B1 into a
**B2 depth** phase and an honest **C1 gateway**. This file is the authoring outline
for weeks 13–18 — the counterpart to plan.md §3, not a replacement. **Do not edit
plan.md.** Schemas (plan.md §2), the block taxonomy, the validator, the weekly
rhythm, and the "mijn verhaal" thread are all unchanged and inherited.*

---

## 0. Honest framing

Twelve weeks got the learner to a real, usable B1: they can hold a conversation,
tell stories across tenses, give opinions, and stay in Dutch. Season 2 does **not**
promise a certificate. It promises the two things that actually separate a good B1
speaker from someone approaching C1:

1. **Depth** — the grammar B1 skips: the full passive, the irreële (hypothetical
   past), formal and written register, long subclause chains, indirect discourse,
   advanced *er*. This is the **B2 Depth** block (weeks 13–16).
2. **Nuance & register agility** — particles layered for effect, irony,
   switching between formal and playful, idioms, and functioning in professional
   and media Dutch at speed. This is the **C1 Gateway** block (weeks 17–18):
   *preparing the leap, not certifying it.* The finale (day 126) names this plainly.

Levels in the manifest: `B2` ("B2 Depth", weeks 13–16) and `C1` ("C1 Gateway",
weeks 17–18). The validator only checks the `level` field is present, so these new
codes drop in cleanly.

## 2. Register notes (new for season 2)

Register is now a *taught object*, not just a background dial. Three registers are
named and drilled:

- **Informeel** — *je/jij*, particles, ellipsis, *joh/hoor/nou*. The default the
  learner already owns.
- **Neutraal/verzorgd** — the register of a decent email to someone you don't know,
  a shop, a landlord. *U*, full sentences, fewer particles.
- **Formeel/geschreven** — official letters, sollicitatie, nominalized style
  (*"na ontvangst van uw bericht"*, *"het verzoek tot"*), passives, no contractions.

The recurring drill (see style-guide §7b): **register-switching** — the *same*
message rendered informally vs formally. Media/news Dutch is treated as
**exposure only**: the learner should *recognise* the compressed news register and
fast speech (and hear one **Flemish** contrast clip for awareness), not reproduce it.

## 3. Curriculum — Weeks 13–18 (days 85–126)

Weekly rhythm unchanged: **5 teaching days + 1 review/immersion + 1 capstone.**
Every capstone ends with the week's **"mijn verhaal"** layer (§4). Every review day
carries the season-2 arc. Duration `[20, 35]`; block-count targets unchanged
(lesson 8–12, review 6–8, capstone 10–14).

### B2 Depth — Weeks 13–16

**Week 13 — M13 · U13 · Het volledige passief** *(the full passive system)* — level **B2**
Grammar spine of the week: *worden/zijn + door*, the action-vs-state split.
85. **Passief met *worden*** — action passive, present/future; *door* + agent; *De brief wordt geschreven*, *Hier wordt gewerkt*.
86. **Passief in het verleden** — *werd* (imperfectum passief) vs *is geworden*; and the classic B2 trap: action passive (*De deur werd geopend*) vs **state/result** passive (*De deur is geopend* = *zijn* + participle).
87. **Passief met modalen & in de bijzin** — *het moet gedaan worden*, *…dat het besproken wordt*; verb-cluster order at clause end.
88. **Het onpersoonlijk passief** — agentless & *er*-passive: *Er wordt gedanst*, *Er werd veel gelachen*; the register of signs and notices.
89. **Passief vs actief — en *men*** — when written Dutch prefers the passive; converting actief↔passief; *men* as the impersonal alternative.
90. **Review + immersion** — *season-2 opener*: Emma's news over coffee; the passive braided into how she describes a reorganisation at work. *(immersion anchor)*
91. **Capstone** — graded passive production + **mijn verhaal (laag 13): a nuanced opinion with connectors** (*hoewel, enerzijds…anderzijds, daarentegen, bovendien*).

**Week 14 — M14 · U14 · De irreële tijd** *(conditional perfect & hypothetical past)* — level **B2**
92. **De voltooid verleden tijd** — pluperfect: *had gedaan*, *was gegaan*; sequencing past events (*nadat ik gegeten had…*).
93. **Zou + infinitief, herzien** — conditional revisited: polite, future-in-past, softening.
94. **De irreële voorwaarde** — *als ik … had geweten, zou ik … hebben gedaan*; the two-clause hypothetical-past machine.
95. **Spijt & verwijt** — regret and reproach: *ik had beter … kunnen doen*, *je had het moeten zeggen*, *was ik maar…*.
96. **Verborgen conditionals** — *anders*, *stel dat*, *mocht het …*; hedged hypotheticals in speech.
97. **Review + immersion** — Daan overthinks the band's support-slot offer entirely in hypotheticals (*als we ja zeggen…, dan had ik…*). *(immersion anchor)*
98. **Capstone** — graded irreële drills + **mijn verhaal (laag 14): a hypothetical/regret about your Dutch journey** (*als ik dit eerder had geweten…*).

**Week 15 — M15 · U15 · Formeel & geschreven Nederlands** *(register & nominalization)* — level **B2**
99. **Het register-schuifje** — informeel / neutraal / formeel; the same message, three ways; *jij→u*, particles out.
100. **Nominalisatie** — verb→noun style: *aankomen → bij aankomst*, *verzoeken → het verzoek tot*; why officialese nominalizes.
101. **De formele e-mail** — openings/closings (*Geachte…, Met vriendelijke groet*), indirect requests, *zou u … kunnen*.
102. **De sollicitatiebrief** — motivation letter: *verantwoordelijk voor*, *ervaring met*, *ik ben van mening dat*; structure & hedged confidence.
103. **Officiële brieven & instanties** — gemeente/Belastingdienst register: *naar aanleiding van*, *bijgaand*, *conform*; decoding, not memorising.
104. **Review + immersion** — Bram helps You draft a formal email; formal↔informal contrast throughout. *(immersion anchor)*
105. **Capstone** — write-a-formal-message drill + **mijn verhaal (laag 15): a professional self-presentation** (formal register, nominalized: *"met ervaring in…", "verantwoordelijk voor…"*).

**Week 16 — M16 · U16 · Bijzin-ketens, relatief & indirecte rede** *(complex syntax + the pivot)* — level **B2**
106. **Bijzin-ketens** — stacking subclauses; verb-final under embedding; keeping the finite verb where it belongs three clauses deep.
107. **Betrekkelijke bijzinnen met *waar* + voorzetsel** — *waarover, waarmee, waarin, waardoor*; *dat/wat* as relative; *degene die*.
108. ***Er*, herzien (advanced)** — *er* stacked with prepositions and quantity in complex clauses (*er zijn er twee die…*, *daar heb ik het niet over gehad*).
109. **Indirecte rede, diep** — reported speech with tense back-shift, *of*-questions, *volgens haar / naar verluidt*; reporting doubt and hearsay.
110. **Discourse-verbanden** — connective adverbs for text cohesion: *daardoor, vandaar, desondanks, immers, althans*.
111. **Review + immersion — THE PIVOT**: Sanne's news lands (she's expecting), delivered and re-told through reported speech + hypotheticals + layered tenses. *(immersion anchor; season-2 life event, mirrors day 69)*
112. **Capstone** — complex-syntax production + **mijn verhaal (laag 16): layered-tense storytelling** — one anecdote that moves across tenses with a reported line (Sanne's news as the model).

### C1 Gateway — Weeks 17–18 *(the leap, honestly framed)*

**Week 17 — M17 · U17 · Nuance: partikels, ironie & register-wisseling** — level **C1**
113. **Partikel-mastery** — *nou/even/toch/wel/maar/eens* **layered** (*"doe nou even rustig"*, *"dat is toch wel zo"*); stacking for exact tone.
114. **Ironie & understatement** — Dutch dryness: *"lekker dan"*, *"nou, geweldig"*, *"niet bepaald"*; hearing sarcasm, and using it safely.
115. **Register-wisseling live** — switching mid-conversation; softening a blunt line, sharpening a soft one; reading the room.
116. **Uitdrukkingen & spreekwoorden, diep** — idioms beyond the literal: *de kat uit de boom kijken, ergens geen kaas van gegeten hebben, met de deur in huis vallen*; using them, not just decoding.
117. **Nuance-werkwoorden & hedging** — *blijken, lijken, schijnen, zou moeten*; *op zich, in principe, eigenlijk, min of meer* — the vagueness a native controls.
118. **Review + immersion** — Daan & Emma banter: irony, idioms and layered particles flying; You keeps up. *(immersion anchor)*
119. **Capstone** — tone/register drills + **mijn verhaal (laag 17): irony & register-switching** — the same point said formally vs playfully, particles layered.

**Week 18 — M18 · U18 · Professioneel & media-Nederlands, en de sprong** — level **C1**
120. **Het werkoverleg** — meeting Dutch: agenda, turn-taking, *ik wil even inhaken, zullen we afronden*, disagreeing politely.
121. **Het sollicitatiegesprek** — interview: STAR-style answers in Dutch, *mijn sterke punt is*, hedged self-praise, questions to ask back.
122. **Vergadertaal & onderhandelen** — proposals, pushback, *daar valt over te praten, laten we een middenweg zoeken*; formal spoken register.
123. **Nieuws-Nederlands (exposure)** — the compressed news register: nominalization, passives, *aldus*, headline grammar; **recognise**, don't reproduce.
124. **Snel & echt Nederlands + Vlaams-contrast (exposure)** — reductions in fast speech (*'k weet 't niet, watte?*), and ONE Flemish contrast clip for ear-awareness (*gij/ge, hesp, goesting* — recognise, stay NL).
125. **Review + immersion** — a real werkoverleg with Youssef, Emma & Bram; professional register at speed; You contributes. *(immersion anchor)*
126. **Finale capstone — Mijn verhaal, C1-gateway** — 18-week recap, the definitive register-agile self-intro, a letter forward to the C1 you, honest framing of the leap, Dutch farewells, celebratory stats. *(mirrors day 84)*

## 5. Difficulty ramp & authoring notes (weeks 13–18)

Full detail in **style-guide.md §7b**. In brief:

- **B2 (W13–16):** prompts and explanations **mixed** Dutch/English, tilting Dutch;
  comprehension questions in Dutch; journal prompts in Dutch, `minSentences` 3+.
- **C1 gateway (W17–18):** **Dutch-first** — prompts, explains and spotlights in
  Dutch, with a short English safety net only where a rule genuinely needs it.
- Dialogues **8–12 lines**; `durationMin` **[20, 35]**.
- **Register-switching drill** (same message formal vs informal) recurs as a pattern.
- Block-count targets **unchanged** — stay inside lesson 8–12 / review 6–8 /
  capstone 10–14 so the validator stays quiet.
- All content **original**, in the cast's world; grammar accuracy non-negotiable
  (passive *worden/zijn* choice, irreële word order, verb-final under deep
  embedding, *er* stacking) — these are exactly the places B2 learners get corrected.
